## Promotion rule

A quote may be enabled only after the specific translation/edition is recorded with:
- exact locator;
- translator/editor where applicable;
- publication/edition data;
- jurisdictionally appropriate rights basis or permission;
- context verification;
- final product-display approval.

Until then the UI may identify that a verified source passage exists, but it must show a summary rather than the exact quotation.
