# Inner Compass — Product Claims Policy

## Allowed positioning

Inner Compass is:
- a guided reflection tool;
- a sourced wisdom browser;
- a deterministic navigation system for reflection themes;
- a general-wellness educational product.

## Do not claim

Do not state or imply that Inner Compass:
- diagnoses a mental-health or medical condition;
- determines whether someone has a disorder;
- treats, cures, mitigates, or prevents a disease;
- replaces therapy, medical care, medication, or professional treatment;
- predicts crisis, relapse, or a health outcome;
- provides a validated clinical assessment or risk score;
- guarantees improved mental health.

## UI wording

Prefer:
- "reflection theme"
- "category match"
- "sourced teaching"
- "general wellness"
- "guided reflection"
- "this is outside Inner Compass's scope"

Avoid in consumer-facing copy:
- "clinical diagnosis"
- "clinical risk marker"
- "detected a disorder"
- "treatment recommendation"
- "patient"
- "therapeutic outcome"

Research documentation may discuss clinical literature, but research language must not automatically become consumer claims.

## Marketing substantiation

Any objective benefit claim added to:
- landing page;
- app store listing;
- social media;
- ads;
- onboarding;
- press materials

must be supported before publication.

High-risk examples requiring separate review:
- “reduces anxiety”
- “improves depression”
- “prevents relapse”
- “clinically proven”
- “therapy alternative”
