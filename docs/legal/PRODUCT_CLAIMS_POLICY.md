# Product Claims Policy

## Allowed positioning

Inner Compass may describe itself as:
- research-informed;
- deterministic;
- source-linked;
- reflective;
- educational/general-wellness;
- designed to support personal reflection and agency.

## Prohibited or restricted claims

Do not claim that Inner Compass:
- diagnoses a condition;
- provides therapy or psychotherapy;
- treats, cures, prevents, or manages a disease or disorder;
- replaces licensed medical or mental-health care;
- guarantees an outcome;
- determines the correct life decision;
- provides emergency response;
- provides legal or financial professional advice.

## Clinical literature

Clinical/psychological literature may inform taxonomy boundaries and safety design. That does **not** make the consumer product a clinical service.

Consumer-facing wording should prefer **research-informed wisdom and reflection** over labels such as “clinical wisdom.”

## Safety routing

When the deterministic safety system reaches a hard ceiling, contemplative guidance must not be presented as a substitute for appropriate human/professional support.

## Source claims

A verified source record means the research provenance was audited. It does not imply endorsement by the historical author, translator, publisher, or institution.
