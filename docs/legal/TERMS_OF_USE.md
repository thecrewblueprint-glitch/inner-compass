# Inner Compass Terms of Use

**Effective:** September 20, 2026  
**Launch scope:** adults 18+ in the United States; English only.

## Product scope

Inner Compass is a deterministic, research-informed general-wellness reflection product. It is designed to support personal reflection and agency.

It is **not** therapy, psychotherapy, diagnosis, medical treatment, legal advice, financial advice, or an emergency-response service. It does not determine the correct life decision for a user.

## Safety

Safety routing runs before ordinary reflection guidance. When a supported safety rule triggers, ordinary wisdom is blocked and the product routes to human-support resources.

Automated language matching has limits. Inner Compass does not promise to recognize every possible phrase, spelling, context, or meaning. The Lifelines surface is always available directly.

## Eligibility

The current release is limited to adults age 18 or older who are located in the United States. The current product and safety-routing experience support English only.

## User responsibility

Users remain responsible for their decisions and for seeking qualified professional or emergency help when appropriate. Inner Compass should not be used to delay urgent human help.

## Third-party resources

External crisis, library, publisher, research, and other links are operated by third parties. Their availability, content, privacy practices, and terms are controlled by those providers.

## Intellectual property

Original Inner Compass software, organization, and original copy are protected by applicable law. Historical/public-domain material and third-party books remain subject to their own rights, attribution, and license status.

## Pre-public-release status

These Terms are a production draft for controlled testing until the legal operator, legal/privacy contact, counsel review, and owner public-launch approval are configured and accepted.

## Contact

The public legal/privacy contact must be configured before public launch. The application intentionally does not invent a placeholder identity or address.
