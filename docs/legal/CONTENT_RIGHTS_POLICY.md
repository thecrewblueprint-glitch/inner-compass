# Content Rights Policy

## Direct quotations

Exact quote display is **off by default**.

A source being old, famous, available online, or research-verified is not enough to authorize product display. Approval is edition/translation-specific.

Required before enabling exact wording:
1. author/work/section verified;
2. translator and edition identified where applicable;
3. surrounding context checked;
4. rights status documented;
5. jurisdictional launch scope considered;
6. product-display approval recorded.

## Research vs product

Research records may retain provenance metadata and rights-safe notes. Modern copyrighted works and translations should generally produce citation + paraphrase rather than a bulk quote corpus.

## Paraphrases

Paraphrases:
- are never placed in quotation marks;
- are never represented as exact historical wording;
- retain source/context linkage.

## Inner Compass affirmations

Affirmations are original Inner Compass wording:
- never attributed as historical quotations;
- linked to audited source records;
- do not claim universal certainty.

## Book covers / website imagery

The Suggested Reads UI must not scrape arbitrary commercial cover art. Records track visual provenance and rights status.

When approved cover art is unavailable, the application renders an original metadata-driven fallback cover/card.
