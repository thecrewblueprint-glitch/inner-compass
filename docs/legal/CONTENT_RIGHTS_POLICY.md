# Inner Compass — Content & Quotation Rights Policy

## Principle

Historical source authenticity and legal display rights are two separate gates.

An ancient source can be public domain while a modern translation remains copyrighted.

## Record types

1. VERIFIED_DIRECT_QUOTE — research record only until product-display rights are separately approved.
2. SOURCE_PARAPHRASE — source-linked summary, not presented as exact words.
3. INNER_COMPASS_AFFIRMATION — original project wording, never attributed as a historical quote.

## Product display rule

Default: **show source-linked teaching summary, not the direct quote.**

A direct quote may enter production only when the exact translation/edition is explicitly marked:
- PRODUCT_DISPLAY_APPROVED_US; or
- PRODUCT_DISPLAY_APPROVED_WORLDWIDE.

`PUBLIC_DOMAIN` in research is not by itself enough for global product display.

## Modern translations

For modern copyrighted translations:
- do not bulk copy;
- do not create a quote library from the translation without permission/license;
- use citation + original paraphrase when appropriate;
- do not rely on an arbitrary word-count rule as a substitute for fair-use analysis.

## Attribution

Product UI must distinguish:
- direct quote;
- teaching summary;
- original Inner Compass reflection.

Never place quotation marks around paraphrases or original affirmations.

## Research promotion checklist

- exact wording verified;
- surrounding context checked;
- author/attribution caveats captured;
- translator identified;
- edition identified;
- jurisdictional rights status checked;
- product-display status explicitly approved;
- safety/category fit approved.
