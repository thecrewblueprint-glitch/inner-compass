# Inner Compass Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Effective:** September 20, 2026

This policy is intentionally separate from the general Privacy Notice because some U.S. state laws regulate consumer health data handled by apps outside traditional healthcare systems.

## Consumer health data a user may enter

A user may choose to type information about mood, stress, relationships, habits, health fears, substance-related concerns, or other sensitive experiences.

## Source

The raw text is provided directly by the user.

## Purpose and processing

The text is processed transiently on the user's device to provide deterministic safety routing, clarification, category matching, and requested reflection content.

## Collection by the operator

The current production architecture is designed so raw reflection text is not received or retained by the Inner Compass operator.

Category-level bookmarks, interaction counts, settings, and privacy-safe diagnostics may exist in local browser storage on the user's device.

## Sharing / sale

The current release does not sell consumer health data and does not intentionally share raw reflection text with third parties.

User-initiated external links are separate interactions with those providers.

## User controls

Users can stop entering text at any time and can clear local Inner Compass data from the Privacy screen.

## Future changes

A future feature that adds accounts, cloud sync, remote reflection processing, analytics, advertising, or a third-party processor requires an updated consumer-health-data analysis, disclosure, and any required consent before release.

## Rights/contact

A public contact and rights-request process must be configured before public launch. This controlled-beta draft does not fabricate an operator identity or contact channel.

## Legal source

Washington My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373.020 requires a clearly and conspicuously disclosed consumer health data privacy policy and a prominent homepage link for covered entities and small businesses:
