# Inner Compass Safety & Crisis Notice

**Scope:** United States, adults 18+, English-only release.

Inner Compass is not an emergency service.

## Routing rule

Every submitted reflection passes through deterministic upstream safety routing before ordinary wisdom/category retrieval.

When a supported safety route triggers, ordinary wisdom is blocked and the product routes to relevant human-support resources.

## Detection limit

Language is variable. Deterministic safety rules can be tested exhaustively against their supported rule/fixture matrix, but no automated text matcher can honestly guarantee recognition of every possible human wording or context.

The production safety claim is therefore:

> For every supported input path, the safety router executes first; for every tested supported safety condition, the expected safety route must trigger; and any triggered/precautionary safety result blocks ordinary wisdom.

## Precautionary routing

Safety-related wording with unclear, indirect, historical, negated, or otherwise ambiguous context is routed conservatively to a safety-review state rather than ordinary wisdom.

## Direct access

Lifelines remain directly accessible without entering reflection text.

## English only

The current safety rules and notices are designed and tested for English input. Non-English safety coverage is not claimed.
