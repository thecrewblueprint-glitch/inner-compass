# Wisdom mapping — v1.0

**Scope.** This is the index over `docs/wisdom-mapping/mapping-*.md`, which maps each of the 25 [`TAXONOMY_V1.0.md`](TAXONOMY_V1.0.md) categories to real, sourced content across three pillars: **(A) Eastern philosophy/metaphysical teaching**, **(B) Jungian/post-Jungian shadow work**, and **(C) evidence-based, non-clinical psychology methodology for building a better life**. This complements — and deliberately doesn't duplicate — the clinical-treatment grounding in [`CLINICAL_KB_V1.0.md`](CLINICAL_KB_V1.0.md).

**A scope note, flagged deliberately.** The app's original framing (see `README.md`'s earlier drafts and the initial taxonomy work) was "Eastern philosophical/metaphysical guidance." This mapping pass, per direction, broadens that to three pillars — Eastern philosophy, shadow work, and evidence-based psychology methodology. That's a real product-scope decision, not just a research addition, so it's called out here rather than absorbed silently. `README.md` has been updated to reflect it; revisit if that's not the intended direction.

**Sourcing discipline.** Every claim in the five mapping files traces to a real text, a real named author/teacher's documented work, or a real peer-reviewed study — never an invented quote or technique. Multiple agents doing this research independently declined to use widely-circulated quotes (several misattributed to Jung) that couldn't be verified against a primary source, substituting verified passages instead. Where a pillar's application to a specific category is a reasonable extrapolation rather than directly evidenced, that's stated explicitly in the source files and carried into the gaps list below — never silently upgraded.

## Safety carryovers (load-bearing — read before this content ships)

These aren't new findings — they're `CLINICAL_KB_V1.0.md`'s safety notes, confirmed to still apply once real wisdom content is attached to each category:

- **#10 Substance use — hard ceiling.** Wisdom content here is only for someone not in active withdrawal or crisis. Never a substitute for the crisis-resource redirect or medical care.
- **#21 Guilt — escalation candidate.** Moral-injury-adjacent guilt (self-condemnation tied to real/perceived serious harm, especially in a caregiving role) is tied to suicide risk in current literature — route to crisis redirect, not a philosophy match.
- **#24 Anhedonia — equal-or-greater priority than sadness language.** Sudden/recent-onset loss of pleasure or interest independently predicts suicidality. Do not treat this category as lower-stakes than #23.
- **#18 Illness/mortality fear — referral edge.** Compulsive health-checking or functional impairment needs professional-support framing alongside impermanence teachings, not instead of it.
- **#19 Financial fear — practical edge.** Acute financial crisis (job loss, imminent eviction) needs real resource pointers alongside philosophy, not philosophy in place of them.
- **#25 Mistreated/bullied — severity tiering.** Chronic, power-imbalanced bullying may present as complex PTSD; pair content with a professional-support signal for severe/chronic cases.
- **#5 Ongoing conflict — a hard boundary surfaced during this pass.** Chödrön's "stay with the relationship" lojong framing must never be applied where the "conflict" is actually intimate partner violence or abuse. That routing decision sits upstream, in the taxonomy's existing IPV crisis-redirect exclusion — this content is only for good-faith, non-abusive conflict.

## Gaps and extrapolations (stated, not papered over)

Consistent with `CLINICAL_KB_V1.0.md`'s own honesty about unresolved gaps, these pillar applications are flagged as extrapolations or unverifiable rather than upgraded to sourced fact:

- **#5 Ongoing conflict, pillar B** — the popular "everything that irritates us about others..." Jung quote is unverified (flagged unsourced on Wikiquote); real CW9ii passages (¶14, ¶17) were cited instead.
- **#6 Shame, pillar B** — a widely-circulated "shame is a soul-eating emotion" Jung quote could not be verified; a real CW11 passage was cited instead.
- **#8 Feeling stuck, pillar C** and **#22 Impatience, pillar C** — "ACT helps with indecision/impatience" is a principled extrapolation from ACT's general mechanism, not a targeted clinical trial (matches the same flag already in `CLINICAL_KB_V1.0.md`).
- **#15 Overwhelm** — the non-workplace "too much to control" framing (wu wei, ACT content) is reasonably extrapolated, not directly clinically validated outside occupational burnout, consistent with ICD-11 scoping burnout as strictly occupational.
- **#19 Financial fear, pillar A** — the popular one-line Tao Te Ching "quote" on contentment could not be verified against a specific scholarly translation; a multi-translation compilation of chapter 44 was cited instead.
- **#20 Envy — Isolation root** — no source ties envy to Yalom's existential isolation specifically (only to social comparison/Beck's cognitive model); stays flagged as the taxonomy's own inference, matching `CLINICAL_KB_V1.0.md`.
- **#22 Impatience, pillar B** — von Franz's puer aeternus material doesn't independently support the Death root; consistent with the clinical KB's own finding that Death is a designed inference here, not sourced.
- **#24 Anhedonia, pillar B** — Jung's libido-withdrawal account of depression is well documented, but no source applies it to anhedonia by that specific clinical name; the application here is a labeled extrapolation.
