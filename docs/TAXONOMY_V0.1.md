# Wisdom-guidance app — problem taxonomy, v0.1

**Methodology, stated plainly.** Build order: draft taxonomy from existing evidence-based frameworks first, then run scoped case-study research per category to validate/refine it, then map philosophical teachings onto the refined categories. This document is step one — a real starting structure pulled from established clinical and psychological categorization, not invented from intuition. Explicitly a draft (v0.1), meant to be split, merged, or renamed once the case-study research pass happens.

## Two-layer structure

### Layer 1 — existential roots

Pulled from Irvin Yalom's *Existential Psychotherapy* (1980): four "ultimate concerns," argued to be the root of essentially all psychological distress.

| Root | Yalom's framing |
|---|---|
| **Death** | We want to live, but know we will die — impermanence, time, loss |
| **Freedom** | We have to choose, with no absolute ground for the choice — responsibility, control, groundlessness |
| **Isolation** | No matter how connected we are, each of us is alone in our own experience |
| **Meaninglessness** | We want life to mean something, but the universe supplies no inherent meaning |

This layer is the natural axis for the philosophy-matching side: Eastern traditions speak directly to exactly these four (impermanence teachings for Death, wu wei/dichotomy-of-control material for Freedom, non-duality/interconnectedness teachings for Isolation, dharma/purpose teachings for Meaninglessness).

### Layer 2 — practical presenting categories

Pulled from Crisis Text Line's published contact categories (stress, anxiety, loneliness, bullying, substance use, self-harm, suicidal thoughts, economic worry, relationships, culture/identity, illness, intimate partner violence, depression), standard clinical intake presenting-concern categories, and Plutchik's eight basic emotions (joy, trust, fear, surprise, sadness, anticipation, anger, disgust) as a secondary emotion-word entry point. This is the layer a user's actual typed-in problem gets matched against first.

## v0.1 category list

| # | Surface category | Maps to root(s) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loneliness / feeling isolated even around people | Isolation |
| 2 | Grief / loss of a person, relationship, or way of life | Death |
| 3 | General anxiety / dread about the future | Freedom, Death |
| 4 | Anger / ongoing conflict with someone | Freedom |
| 5 | Shame / feeling fundamentally not okay | Meaninglessness, Isolation |
| 6 | Rejection / heartbreak | Isolation, Meaninglessness |
| 7 | Feeling stuck / can't decide | Freedom |
| 8 | Fear of failure or of a specific outcome | Freedom, Death |
| 9 | Substance use / compulsive coping | Death, Meaninglessness |
| 10 | Betrayal / broken trust | Isolation |
| 11 | Feeling like nothing I do matters / career or life purpose | Meaninglessness |
| 12 | Overwhelm / too much to control | Freedom |
| 13 | Identity or belonging confusion | Meaninglessness, Isolation |
| 14 | Illness or fear about health / mortality | Death |
| 15 | Financial or security fear | Freedom, Death |
| 16 | Jealousy / comparing myself to others | Isolation, Meaninglessness |
| 17 | Guilt / can't forgive myself | Freedom, Meaninglessness |
| 18 | Impatience / waiting for something to change | Freedom, Death |
| 19 | Low mood / can't feel joy | all four |
| 20 | Being mistreated / bullied / disrespected | Isolation, Freedom |

Deliberately **not included in v0.1**: active suicidal ideation, self-harm, intimate partner violence, and acute psychiatric crisis. Those need a hard, immediate redirect to real crisis resources built into the app before anything else, not a philosophy lookup — a v1 safety requirement, separate from this taxonomy.
