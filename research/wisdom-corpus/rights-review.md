# Rights / Copyright Review

Every source used for a direct user-facing quote must have a rights determination.

## Status values

- PUBLIC_DOMAIN
- OPEN_LICENSE
- LICENSED_OR_PERMISSIONED
- CITATION_ONLY
- REVIEW_REQUIRED

---

Modern copyrighted translations may be excellent research sources while still being unsuitable for storage as a large direct-quote corpus. In those cases, store a citation and a faithful paraphrase instead.
