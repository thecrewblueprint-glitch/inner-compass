# Translation Review Log

## Phase 1 — Stoicism

### [TR-STOIC-001] Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VI.6
- Tradition: Stoicism / Roman Stoicism
- Work: Meditations
- Passage locator: VI.6
- Original-language note: Greek phrase commonly rendered with a verb covering retaliation/avenging/defending oneself.
- Translation A: George Long — “The best way of avenging thyself is not to become like [the wrong-doer].”
- Translator / edition: George Long, 1862
- Translation B: Modern translations often use “revenge” and “become like them.”
- Material wording difference: “Avenging” can sound broader than the underlying ethical point of refusing imitation of wrongdoing.
- Effect on philosophical meaning: Core meaning is stable: do not reproduce the wrongdoer's character in retaliation.
- Effect on category mapping: Strong for anger/mistreatment; weaker for betrayal repair.
- Preferred research translation: George Long for public-domain direct quotation.
- Preferred user-facing treatment: Quote may be shown with a plain-language teaching summary and safety boundary.
- Rights implications: Long translation public domain in the U.S.

### [TR-STOIC-002] Epictetus, Enchiridion 1
- Tradition: Stoicism / Roman Stoicism
- Work: Enchiridion
- Passage locator: 1
- Original-language note: Modern scholarship often renders the distinction as what is “up to us” rather than literally “in our control.”
- Translation A: George Long — “Of things some are in our power, and others are not.”
- Translator / edition: George Long, 1890 edition
- Translation B: Common modern rendering — “Some things are up to us and some are not.”
- Material wording difference: “In our power/control” can be interpreted as stronger causal control than Stoic prohairesis implies.
- Effect on philosophical meaning: Retrieval notes should emphasize response/judgment/choice rather than total control of outcomes.
- Effect on category mapping: Strong for anxiety, overwhelm, indecision, impatience; use cautiously for financial/health issues.
- Preferred research translation: George Long for public-domain quote storage.
- Preferred user-facing treatment: Pair with an explanation of practical action and external constraints.
- Rights implications: Public domain.
- Review status: SOURCE_VERIFIED; independent promotion audit pending.

### [TR-STOIC-003] Seneca, Moral Letters 13.4
- Tradition: Stoicism / Roman Stoicism
- Work: Moral Letters to Lucilius
- Passage locator: 13.4
- Original-language note: Latin “opinione” can be rendered as imagination, apprehension, or opinion.
- Translation A: Richard M. Gummere — “We suffer more often in imagination than in reality.”
- Translator / edition: Gummere, Loeb Vol. I, 1917
- Material wording difference: “Imagination” can sound like the fear is fabricated; “apprehension” better preserves anticipatory fear.
- Effect on philosophical meaning: Do not use to dismiss known threats.
- Effect on category mapping: Strong for anticipatory anxiety and fear of failure.
- Preferred research translation: Gummere, with context note.
- Preferred user-facing treatment: Short quote plus “anticipatory fear” explanation.
- Rights implications: Early Loeb edition treated as public domain in U.S. sources used here.
- Review status: SOURCE_VERIFIED; independent promotion audit pending.

### [TR-STOIC-004] Seneca, Letter 28.1
- Tradition: Stoicism / Roman Stoicism
- Work: Moral Letters to Lucilius
- Passage locator: 28.1
- Original-language note: Gummere's “change of soul” is idiomatic and may read metaphysically to modern users.
- Translation A: Richard M. Gummere — “You need a change of soul rather than a change of climate.”
- Material wording difference: Modern translations may use “mind” or “self.”
- Effect on philosophical meaning: The argument is that external relocation alone does not resolve internally carried patterns.
- Effect on category mapping: Moderate for identity confusion/low mood; unsafe-environment exceptions are essential.
- Preferred research translation: Gummere, with explanatory note.
- Preferred user-facing treatment: Avoid if relocation is protective or materially necessary.
- Rights implications: Public domain U.S. edition.
- Review status: SOURCE_VERIFIED; independent promotion audit pending.
