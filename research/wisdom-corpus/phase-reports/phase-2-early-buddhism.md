## Editorial source-policy decision

Bhikkhu Sujato / SuttaCentral translations are legally published under CC0, but the translator explicitly asks that the translations not be used in AI training, datasets, or AI-derived technologies. Inner Compass respects that request.

Result:
- no Sujato wording is stored as product direct-quote corpus material in this phase
- SuttaCentral may be used only as a translation/context reference if needed
- older public-domain translations are preferred for direct quote storage

## Translation issues documented

- Dhammapada 5: Müller's “love” vs modern “non-hatred/non-hostility”
- Dhammapada 160: “Self” must not be mistaken for an atman doctrine
- Dhammapada 277: “created things perish” is a dated rendering of conditioned impermanence
- Udana 1.10: Strong's wording is not suitable for direct product quotation without translation comparison
- Sutta-Nipata 1.3: renunciant solitude must not be universalized into ordinary loneliness advice
- SuttaCentral/Sujato: excluded from direct corpus use by editorial respect for translator request

## Rights issues documented

- Müller, Fausböll, Chalmers, Strong, and Moore editions are treated as public domain in the United States
- Samyutta/Woodward direct wording is withheld in this phase because the consulted electronic transcription asserts commercial rights
- international rights remain a separate deployment audit

## Safety / category-boundary decisions

- no Buddhist teaching may bypass the Category 10 substance-use hard ceiling
- impermanence is not a timetable for grief or low mood
- goodwill/non-hatred does not require proximity, reconciliation, or remaining in unsafe relationships
- contemplative attention is not clinical treatment
- contentment must not romanticize illness, poverty, or unmet basic needs
- the Rhinoceros discourse is retained chiefly as a boundary/context record, not as a recommendation to socially isolate
