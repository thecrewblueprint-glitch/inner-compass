## Textual-integrity rules adopted

- The received Daodejing is treated as a composite textual tradition; records use “Laozi (traditional attribution)” rather than presenting single authorship as settled fact.
- The received Zhuangzi is treated as multi-authored and layered.
- Zhuangzi records identify Inner vs Outer chapter location.
- Outer Chapter 20 is not attributed directly to the historical Zhuang Zhou.
- Modern scholarship is used for context/authorship cautions, not as direct-quote corpus material.

## Important boundaries

- wu wei is not rendered as “do nothing”
- softness/flexibility is not submission to harm
- contentment does not replace practical financial/resource support
- “uselessness” does not erase concrete responsibilities
- the butterfly dream is not presented as “reality is unreal”
- Zhuangzi identity material is not used for destabilizing reality-testing symptoms
- the empty-boat story is used only when intent is ambiguous; it must not erase known wrongdoing, abuse, or accountability
- philosophical attention practices are not presented as clinical treatment
